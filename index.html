Problem 1: Ulric's $40 (strip diagram)

Ulric was given $40. He saved half in his savings account. He will spend the rest over the next 4 days, the same amount each day. Which strip diagram shows a way to find d, the dollars he spends each day?
  1. Half of $40 is saved: 40 ÷ 2 = 20.
  2. The rest to spend: 40 − 20 = 20.
  3. Spread over 4 days: 4 × d = 20, so d = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.

The correct diagram shows a part of 20 (saved) plus four equal d parts (spent), for a total of 40. That is G:

40
Answer: G (d = $5 each day)

Problem 9: Which equation does the model show?

The model shows dots grouped in boxes of 6, with a few dots left over. Which equation does it represent?

Counting the model: 7 full boxes of 6 dots, and 5 dots left outside any box.

66666665 left
  1. 7 × 6 = 42 dots in the boxes.
  2. 42 + 5 = 47 dots in total.
  3. So the equation is 47 ÷ 6 = 7 r 5.
A  47 ÷ 6 = 6 r 3
B  47 ÷ 6 = 7 r 5
C  47 ÷ 6 = 6 r 11
D  47 ÷ 6 = 7 r 11

A remainder can never be 6 or more when dividing by 6, so C and D (remainder 11) are impossible. A gives 6 × 6 + 3 = 39, not 47. Check B: 7 × 6 + 5 = 47 ✔

Answer: B (47 ÷ 6 = 7 r 5)
